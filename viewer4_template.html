MRI volume

GNM head fitted to the MRI skin surface: scale 0.932 from the mid-sagittal face profile, then rotation/translation, head-shape and lower-face expression coefficients (regularised) with correspondences in both directions. Grey = outside the scanned region (MRI slab is only 100 mm wide; top of head and back of neck are outside the field of view), so there is nothing to compare against there. The back of the head is also dark in the MRI (coil sensitivity), so the MRI surface there is unreliable. Slice sliders move through the full 3D volume. Orange = boundary of the solid tissue (signal above 0.25) facing the spaces inside the head, below the brain and in front of the spine: tongue, palate, velum, pharynx and nasal walls. Built like the skin outline; flat cut planes are removed. Teeth and bone are dark like air, so they show up as part of the spaces.
