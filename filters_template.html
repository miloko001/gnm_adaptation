The filter is applied first; then, live in the page: voxels brighter than the tissue threshold are solid; an opening removes solid specks and thin bridges; the remaining space is split into connected pieces, and a piece is kept if it is bigger than the min size, contains voxels darker than the dark-seed level (real air, not just faint tissue) and is centred within the midline limit. The wireframe is the boundary of the kept space.

Slices

Filters run on the box around the tract (below the brain, in front of the spine); outside it the slices show the unfiltered scan. Flat faces where the space meets the box are left out of the wireframe.
