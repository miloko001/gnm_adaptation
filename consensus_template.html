Each scan is thresholded at every tissue level between the lowest and highest; a point's score is the fraction of scans × levels in which it is empty space. Agreement is the score needed to be part of the surface: high keeps only space that is open for every sound (fixed tubes), low adds space open for only some sounds. Low thresholds give cleanly bounded spaces; high ones also open up light articulator contact and faint artefacts. Symmetry blends the map with its mirror image about the head's midline plane (0 = as measured, 1 = fully left–right averaged).
